# Privacy Policy

## Overview

Bible is a public project maintained by Igor Osipov and made available through:

- GitHub: `https://github.com/nicolapitersky/Bible`
- Website: `https://www.osipov.uk/Bible`

This Privacy Policy describes the intended privacy position for the public project website and related project contact points.

## Website data collection

At present, the Bible website is intended to be a simple public informational site and documentation mirror.

The intended default position is:

- no account creation
- no user login
- no newsletter signup
- no contact form on the Bible site itself
- no analytics configured specifically for the Bible site
- no advertising
- no deliberate tracking or profiling of visitors

## Cookies

The project does not intend to set its own tracking or marketing cookies for the Bible website.

If the hosting platform, browser, or embedded third-party services introduce their own technical behaviour, those may be governed by their own policies rather than by this project.

## Repository and platform activity

If you interact with the project through GitHub, your activity is also governed by GitHub’s own privacy practices and platform terms.

This includes actions such as:

- viewing the repository
- opening issues
- submitting pull requests
- starring, forking, or watching the repository
- commenting or participating in discussions

## Direct contact

If you contact the maintainer directly by email or through another direct channel, the information you provide may be used for purposes such as:

- responding to your message
- handling support requests
- reviewing contributions or collaboration requests
- responding to security or governance matters

The project does not state any intention to sell personal data or use it for unrelated marketing purposes.

## Minimal data approach

The intended operating approach for Bible is data minimisation.

The project aims to avoid collecting personal data unless it is voluntarily provided through direct communication or inherently created through use of third-party platforms such as GitHub or LinkedIn.

## Third-party services

This project may reference or link to third-party services, including:

- GitHub
- LinkedIn
- the main website at `https://www.osipov.uk`

Those third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the publication model changes, including if the site later adds:

- analytics
- forms
- mailing list features
- comments
- embedded third-party services
- account or community functionality
